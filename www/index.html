GDU About & History

GDU™ Good Drivers Unite - About the Idea; You Need to ask yourself 3 basic questions:

  • Do You Drive?
  • Do You own a Smart Phone with Data Enabled?
  • Do U think of yourself as a responsible driver?

If you've answered Yes to above 3 Questions, Consider Joining Voluntary Good Driver’s Association - GDU™

  • All GDU needs from you is your Email Address, which is necessary to open and validate your GDU Account.
  • Driving Rewards will be sent to lucky good-drivers via email e-Transfer, possibly by designated Trust Law Firm.
  • You don’t have to share your real name.
  • Your Car, Age, Gender are nice to have information, but if you want, you could be Batman driving a Batmobile…
  • GDU will not share your driving record without your written consent.
  • Your Driving Record is measured in School Grade Scale Format:

    • City Driving Grade Scale:

    40kph < A ≤ 55kph < B ≤ 65kph < C ≤ 75kph < D ≤ 90kph

    HWY Driving Grade Scale:

    90kph < A ≤ 110kph < B ≤ 120kph < C ≤ 130kph < D ≤ ∞kph

  • Only non-erroneous speed Data is Captured.
  • GDU will show You or your household teenage driver/s, how good of a driver are you.
  • GDU will also help You to become a more responsible road user.

Most Important Facts about the GDU idea:

  • To Join & Benefit all you need is a Smart Phone with data enabled and a car that you use day to day
  • You remain in full control of your Driver Record.
  • Your insurance co. will soon make U join their Good Driver – App/Plan, which they will control/administer (Do you really want to depend on that?)
  • If you are a Responsible-Good-Driver, You can win Serious Monetary Rewards:
    • Best Driver of the Month $500.00

    • Depending if the GDU association takes off, other more serious rewards will be offered to Top Drivers on Annual Basis.

GDU™ - History of the Idea

  • While I drove for many years on HWY401 from West To East sides of Toronto. In 2011 an idea struck me that with the Smartphones, there has to be a way to help Drivers to Drive Better, Safer & More Efficient.
  • This is How the Premise of Good Drivers Unite – Drivers Association was invented.
  • The Insurance business Companies were in hot pursuit, and in year or two they had their apps running, with AVIVA in the UK.
    • The Key difference between Insurance Drivers Apps and GDU is that GDU is a Good Driver’s Association, a bit like Drivers Facebook; instead of Individual Insurance Company Better Driver App/s, which only ties you to that one Insurance Company.
    • Also, Available Insurance smart phone Apps utilize Good Driver Algorithms that tend to be unnecessarily over-complicated; while GDU App is simple and focusses only on One-Thing that Kills and is at the root of bad driving, The Speed.
    • Speed Dictates everything, if your speed is in-check, then everything else about your driving will also be in good standing.
    • GDU name came about Quick with the Logo, that was first drawn on piece of paper by hand and then followed by one drawn in MS-Paint.

What is about?

If you are a regular driver, own a smart phone and think of yourself as a responsible Driver, GDU, GoodDriversUnite, is the reward application for you. GDU is a voluntary drivers application which will help you to remain a good driver or will incent you to become a more responsible driver via Driver Self Monitoring - GDU App. GDU, incents you to Drive Safe with a Weekly Best Driver award of $100 & monthly awards of $500, $300, and $200, for 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place, respectively. All paid via email transfer, after winners get assessed.
There is absolutely nothing for you to do, except a one-time quick download of the Free App (iOS), and keep driving safe as you normally do… After each week, if you drive enough and at various speeds, you will get an A/B/C/D Type Driving Grade Report, which will be sent to you via a short email message. In your First week, drive as you normally do, and see what Grade you get, if you get A B/C/ or D, then there is room to improve. If you get an A, then you can consider yourself a strong candidate to win either best weekly driver or best Monthly Driver Award or Both.
Beside the monetary rewards possibilities of good Drivers Awards, It is even more important to know that You and people like You, with the help of the - GDU App, will contribute to make our speed demon-ridden dangerous roads safer and also less congested to everyone, via more careful & responsible driving habits… Slowing down is not only beneficial from a safety perspective but it lowers your Gasoline Consumption. Countless Statistical Road Safety Studies, have shown that one key factor that contributes the most to accident fatalities is driving Speed. It is simply Physics. Lower Your Speed and you may avoid or survive an accident, Arrive Alive to You All…
